Pieces of Nature

Jigsaw puzzles of wild places

Free on the App Store

Pieces of Nature is a calm jigsaw puzzle app for iPad and iPhone. Every puzzle is an openly licensed nature photograph from Wikimedia Commons, credited to its photographer.

  • A new puzzle every day, and about 350 photos to choose from
  • 6 to 150 pieces on iPhone, up to 600 on iPad
  • Puzzles save automatically, every move
  • Preview, edges only, hints, tidy up and a ghost guide when you want help
  • No accounts, no ads and no tracking

Questions? Visit Support. Curious what the app does with your data? Nothing; see the privacy policy.
