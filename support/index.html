Support

Pieces of Nature is a jigsaw puzzle app for iPad and iPhone with openly licensed nature photographs from Wikimedia Commons.

Contact

Email support@piecesofnature.app with questions, bug reports or ideas. Include your device model and iPadOS or iOS version if something is not working. We read every message.

Common questions

How do I continue a puzzle I left?
Puzzles save automatically, every move. Unfinished puzzles appear on the Continue shelf at the top of the gallery. Tap one to carry on, or touch and hold it and choose Remove from Continue.
Can I have more than one puzzle going?
Yes, one per photo. If you tap a photo that already has a puzzle in progress, its start sheet offers to continue it or start a new one.
What is the daily puzzle?
Every day there is a new puzzle at the top of the gallery, with the same photo and the same cut for everyone. Finish it on consecutive days to build a streak. Tap the calendar to play days you missed.
How do hints work?
Tap the lightbulb and a piece glides to where it belongs, joining its neighbour. Each puzzle has three hints (two on 6-piece puzzles); the badge shows how many are left.
How do I play without a connection?
Touch and hold a scene in the gallery (such as Waterfalls) and choose Keep offline; collections can be kept offline too. Its photos download in the background and stay on the device. See and remove downloads in Settings, from the gear button in the gallery.
What are collections?
Hand-picked sets of photos, such as Golden hour or Iceland, each with a short introduction. They appear on a shelf in the gallery.
How do I change the number of pieces?
Tapping a photo opens its start sheet, where you pick the piece count: 6 to 150 on iPhone, and up to 600 on iPad. Your best time for each count is shown beside it. A puzzle keeps the piece count it was started with.
What do the toolbar buttons do?
Preview shows the whole photo. Edges only dims the middle pieces. The lightbulb is a hint. The eye cycles the ghost guide (a faint image of the finished picture on the table) between off, faint and strong. Tidy up moves loose pieces to the sides of the table. Shuffle scatters the puzzle and starts it over. After a few seconds the bar folds into a small pill at the top; tap it to bring the bar back, or turn on Keep puzzle controls open in Settings.
Where is the timer?
Your time is always kept and shown when you finish. To see it while you play, turn on Show timer while playing in Settings.
What is the gray shape on the table?
It shows the size and place of the finished puzzle. A piece or cluster dropped close to its right place inside it locks there.
On iPhone the pieces are small.
Pinch to zoom the table. One finger on empty table pans, and one finger on a piece drags it.
A photo will not open.
The first time you open a photo it is downloaded, so you need a connection. Four photos are built into the app and always work offline, as do scenes you have chosen to keep offline. Pull down on the gallery to refresh the library.
Who took these photos, and where?
When you finish a puzzle, the photo's story shows a few lines about the place, the photographer and, where known, a map pin. Tap the info button in the gallery for the photographer, licence and a link to the original on Wikimedia Commons for every photo.
Does the app collect my data?
No. See the privacy policy.
